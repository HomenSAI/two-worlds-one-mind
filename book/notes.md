## "Homo roboticus" — prior use of the term

The phrase *Homo roboticus* is not new. It appears, for example, in books (2017, 2019, 2023), in an
*Anthropology News* article (2018) and in academic writing on human extinction and space. The author
does not claim to have coined the phrase. What this book puts forward as the author's own is the
**vision built around it**: life carried by machines through the Universe; consciousness transfer
as the only way for an individual to travel there; "the less routine, the more ideas"; the
break-it-down approach to uncertainty.

## Related ideas by others

- **Hans Moravec**, *Mind Children: The Future of Robot and Human Intelligence* (1988) — machines
  as the successors of human minds.
- **John von Neumann** — self-replicating machines; the idea later applied to space probes that
  copy themselves from local material.
- **Albert Einstein** — "Imagination is more important than knowledge" (interview, 1929).

## Science facts used

- Voyager 1: launched 1977, in interstellar space since 2012, still transmitting.
- Milky Way: about 100,000 light-years across.
