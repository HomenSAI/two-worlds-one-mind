# Contributing

This is a book, not a software project. The most useful help is a correction.

## What to send

- a **factual error** (with a source),
- a **weak or unclear sentence** (with a suggestion),
- a **translation problem** in the German edition.

Use the issue form *Text, fact or translation correction* (Issues → New issue). It asks for the
chapter, the quotation, the problem, your suggestion and a source.

## Please include

- the language edition (EN or DE) and the chapter;
- the exact sentence you mean;
- a source for facts: author, title, year, and a link, DOI or ISBN.

## Please do not post

Personal data (yours or anyone else's), contracts or client documents, passwords or keys.
Do not paste copyrighted text beyond a short quotation.

## Languages

Issues and pull requests may be in English, German or Russian. The book itself is edited in English
first; the German edition follows the English text (see
[docs/translation-status.md](docs/translation-status.md)).

## Pull requests

Small pull requests for typos and link fixes are welcome. For anything larger, open an issue first.
Before opening a pull request, run the checks: `./scripts/check.sh` (see
[docs/build.md](docs/build.md)). Contributions to the text are accepted under the
licence of the book, [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE).

The author decides what goes into the book. AI assistance in writing the book is disclosed in
[Notes](book/notes.md#about-this-text).
