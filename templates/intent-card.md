# Intent card (template)

[Deutsch](intent-card.de.md) · From [Field guide, section 1](../book/08-field-guide.md#1-before-you-start-the-intent-card) ·
A filled-in example: [examples/order-table](../examples/order-table/README.md)

Copy this page, fill it in before you build anything — with people, with AI, or alone. Do not
put personal data, customer data or secrets into a card that you share or publish.

```text
INTENT CARD
-----------
Date / version:

What do I want?
  (one sentence, no technical words)

Why?
  (what changes when it exists)

For whom?

What does "done" look like?
  (a measurable check: a weight, a time, a number, a test. If you cannot write it, you are not
  ready to delegate.)

What must NOT happen?
  (safety, money, data, deadline)

What do I already know?
  (data, drawings, examples, old projects)

What is uncertain?
  (list it - that is where you start)

How will I check it?
  (who or what verifies; what I need to understand to judge the result)

If a step fails its check, I will:  [ ] research   [ ] change the requirements   [ ] stop
  (write down why)
```

Before handing the card to an AI tool, also read [Field guide, section 6](../book/08-field-guide.md#6-know-where-your-data-goes):
know where your data goes.
