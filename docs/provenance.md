## Notes

- Two states of the site are named in documents: **2.11**, the historical basis of edition 1.0, and
  **2.15**, the version seen by the 5 October 2026 audit. They are different states; no comparison
  of the book with either site version was made for edition 1.1. If the site text is to be
  reflected in the book, do the comparison first and record it as a new row.
- The Vision essay is archived separately (Zenodo, version DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23119816`, concept
  DOI `10.5281/zenodo.23119815`, published 2026-10-03, English and German PDF). It is a different
  work from this book. In the Zenodo record, `homensai.com/vision.html` is listed as the page the
  essay was derived from.
- Frozen state: published tags are never moved. Newer text is released as a new edition.
