## What a review should look at

1. Tone: the English text is first-person and informal; the German uses *ich* and *Sie* in a
   professional register. Check that the author's voice survived.
2. Terms: *Absichtskarte*, *Praxisleitfaden*, *mechanische Systeme*, *Weitblick / Weite des Blicks*
   (used for "breadth of vision"), *lokale KI*.
3. Numbers and quotations: figures and the Einstein quotation (translated from the English wording).
4. Statements about the author's career: they are the author's own; the translator did not verify them.

When a review has been done, record who, when and what changed in the table above and in the
[CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).
